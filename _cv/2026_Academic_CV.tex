\begin{rSection}{Research Interests}

\textbf{Track 1 -- Human--AI Collaboration}: I study the design and economics of human--AI collaboration, with particular emphasis on delegation, decision fusion, coordination, and complementary intelligence in hybrid human--AI teams.

\textbf{Track 2 -- AI Strategy and the Economics of Innovation}: I examine how generative AI alters firms' sources of competitive advantage, reorganizes innovation processes, and changes how value is created and captured.

\textbf{Methods}: Formal analytical modeling; structural modeling and estimation; computational simulation, including agent-based computational models and LLM-based generative-agent simulations; and design science research.
\end{rSection}
